# 0.4.0 candidate benchmarks, 2 November 2026

These are the completed pre-release measurements used for the 0.4.0 benchmark handoff.
They measure candidate source `8a7fd82f1c562025a8d94930faf885f4a93e6b5b`, tree
`a388ea1de05e3dd06d12a66f7aafb8cd9ee9c67d`. They are not measurements of the later
release commit `22fa92223d7b53d8efd01f31bfebfd42d0fb5e0e` or its published package.

## Method and scope

Engine and field observations ran on the same Omarchy minipc on 2 November 2026,
kernel `7.2.8-9-omarchy`. The 0.3.12 engine baseline is the frozen installed-package copy
at source `f21789e56cc4b0877caa3156fff7bce52a8dd9c2`. Its backend SHA256 is
`7b7c4f58c3a50681a88e051e8029fe38d5a67613a5671e424849a4888d564ba2`.
Three interleaved cold samples per build measure launch to Hyprland's window-open event;
GUI + backend PSS and resident GPU memory are sampled at map plus three seconds.

Field A reuses nine unchanged rival arms from the prior run only after the host, kernel,
boot/display/GPU session, power, package versions, executable hashes and empty-file fixture
matched. The three Flea samples are fresh. Rival rows retain their original identities and
dates. Flea uses List, distro rivals use their installed icon/grid views, and the four
Strata arms state stable/nightly and columns/list explicitly. This fixture does not measure
thumbnail throughput or equal visible painting work. Mapping is compositor registration;
settling uses the unchanged helper-inclusive CPU-quiet endpoint. See the [field method](../benchmarks.md).

Search ran on the separate `native2` Omarchy machine, also kernel `7.2.8-9-omarchy`.
Its fixture has one million entries and SHA256
`12659de35e410af3177d581531ce936b1dfa41fb1f085f03119e5b8c6423b915`.
Timing starts at `searchopen` and ends at the first nonempty current-generation backend
IPC rows. Constructor/process setup and initial listing are outside the clock. PSS is
sampled after index landing, before queries and audits. The original GAP80 comparator
admits Flea first of four for both listed engine metrics. The retained p95 disposition
does not grant an additional GUI, all-query or end-to-end launch claim.

Quality remains separate from those metric ranks. Flea and nd place all five final quality
targets at rank 1; fff returns all five in full results but four in its reported head;
FSearch returns four of five in full results. Mixed typed-first scores are Flea 39.394%,
nd 48.485%, fff 56.566% and FSearch 29.293%. All measured no-match controls returned zero hits.

Field B was collected in one completed 30-sample phase, but completing collection does
not make every observation eligible. Flea has three valid mapping, settling and CPU samples,
and only two valid PSS samples. Its partial PSS range exceeds the complete Thunar and Nemo
ranges. PCManFM's bounded noncompletion, Strata's render-watcher mismatch, and missing
Nautilus/Dolphin helper PSS prevent a complete seven-family ranking. Zero watcher output
does not establish zero decoded thumbnails. The public media CSV retains every refusal.

Nine of thirteen comparisons are admitted under these scopes. Four media comparisons
remain partial or refused. These are machine- and fixture-specific measurements with
observed ranges; no new measurements or package-installation claims are added here.

## README version format

The README shows numeric base versions as three components, omitting package revisions
and development suffixes. A missing patch component is displayed as zero: Nautilus 51.1
becomes 51.1.0, and FSearch 0.3 becomes 0.3.0. Exact package/build versions and measured
commit identities remain unchanged in the CSVs. The measured FSearch commit
`c20e6b17bf4b56ddf8b56bf021429c5088346ff8` declares version `0.3` in its
[project definition](https://github.com/cboxdoerfer/fsearch/blob/c20e6b17bf4b56ddf8b56bf021429c5088346ff8/meson.build).
